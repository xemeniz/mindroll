HOW TO USE

1. Put it on the vocal track
The vocal goes through untouched.
2. Get the slice
Drop a one-shot or a vocal slice, or press CAPTURE with the song playing: it records the next beat of the track.
3. Draw the speed
The line sets how fast the slice repeats, from 1/2 up to 1/64. It is not locked to the grid: the roll drifts off the beat as it speeds up. Drag the points, click the line to add one, double-click to remove it. LINEAR, EXPO and LATE are starting shapes.
4. Shape the sound
LENGTH in bars. GATE START and END set how much of the slice plays on each hit. PITCH and FILTER rise to the drop. REVERSE turns the last hit around.
5. Listen and drag
PLAY / PAUSE and STOP. Drag the bar at the bottom into Ableton and put the clip right before the drop.

HOW TO USE

1. Put it on a track
Usually the vocal. The track goes through untouched.
2. Get the sound
Drop a one-shot or a vocal slice, or press CAPTURE and play the song: it records the next beat of the track. Drag the two handles on the waveform to choose the part that plays.
3. Draw the speed
The line sets how fast the slice repeats, from 1/2 up to 1/64. It is not locked to the grid: the roll drifts off the beat as it speeds up. Drag the points, click the line to add one, double-click to remove it. LINEAR, EXPO and LATE are starting shapes.
4. Shape the sound
LENGTH from 1 to 32 bars. GATE START and END set how much of the slice plays on each hit. PITCH and FILTER rise to the drop. REVERSE turns the last hit around.
5. Listen and drag
PLAY / PAUSE (or the space bar) and STOP. Drag the bar at the bottom into Ableton and put the clip right before the drop.
